Bio

I am a writer and communications leader in Silicon Valley who creates accessible, compelling stories about complex ideas. My work as Head of Communications and Engagement at Adobe Research focuses on science, technology, and the innovative people who are changing the world with their discoveries.

I write and speak on ethics and practical philosophy. I am especially interested in exploring how ancient Stoic ideas can help us live better today. Subscribe to my Substack, The Stoic Mom, to learn more about how Stoicism can help parents and kids!

My book Beyond Stoicism: A Guide to the Good Life with Stoics, Skeptics, Epicureans, and Other Ancient Philosophers, co-authored with Massimo Pigliucci and Gregory Lopez, was published in January 2025. In the UK, our book was published as Live Like a Philosopher: What the Ancient Greeks and Romans Can Teach Us About Living a Happy Life. Editions in Spanish, Korean, and Chinese are coming soon!

Featured In



I'm also a Certified Professional Co-Active Coach (CPCC), with an approach rooted in Stoic principles. I help coaching clients set meaningful goals and find ways to work towards them. Many of my clients are focused on growing their personal leadership.

My writing has been published in Newsweek, The San Francisco Chronicle, The Industry Standard, California Lawyer, Stanford Business, Stanford Report, Biomedical Computation Review, San Francisco Daily Journal, Los Angeles Daily Journal, Artweek, and more. I've been interviewed on podcasts, spoken at international conferences, and appeared as a guest on NPR-affiliate radio. And ever since I could hold a pencil, I've also pursued creative writing - especially poetry. My book of poetic memoir is called Words That Carry Us.

​I earned my undergraduate degree at Harvard College and my master's at Stanford University. I'm a member of the Stoa Nova school for a new Stoicism, and an external member of the Centre for the Study and Application of Stoicism, University of London, Royal Holloway.
